Chapter 1

What EDI360 does

EDI360 shows every EDI document your business exchanges, from the moment it reaches Orderful to the moment it lands in your ERP, or the other way round, on one screen.

When a trading partner sends you a purchase order, it passes through several systems. Without a single view, finding out where a document is means logging into each one. EDI360 collects the activity from Orderful and Workato and presents each document as a short journey with a clear status at every step.

How a document travels

What you can do with it

Find and follow
Search any document by PO, shipment or invoice number, Orderful transaction id, control number or Workato job id and see exactly where it stands.
Understand a failure
See which step stopped, why, and open the document in Orderful for the exact validation errors.
Stay ahead of acknowledgments
Track 997s per partner, and get reminder emails when one is overdue.
Work an issue
Raise a Jira or ServiceNow ticket from a document and follow its status without leaving EDI360.
Ask in plain language
Use the assistant to look things up, or Explain EDI to read a document in everyday words.
Onboard and test partners
Check each test document end to end, and build test files for a partner.

Chapter 6

A single document in detail

The document page tells the full story of one transaction.

The three blocks

At the top are three blocks in business order. For an inbound document: Orderful, then Workato, then Target (ERP). For an outbound document: Source (ERP), then Workato, then Orderful. Each block has its own status and colour, and an arrow takes the colour of the block it leaves. The first block that is red or amber is usually where to look.

What else is on the page

Business detailsShip from, ship to and sold to, document date, amounts, references and the first line items, read from the document itself.

TimelineEach event in order: received, processed, sent, acknowledged.
RunsEvery time the document was processed, with its status, start time and duration. A retry in Workato appears as another run of the same document.

Buttons on the page

ButtonWhen it appears and what it does
Create ticketRaises a Jira or ServiceNow ticket for this document.
Send emailEmails a report of this document.

Outbound documents and Orderful

For a document you send, the Workato log shows what was sent but not whether Orderful accepted it. EDI360 asks Orderful itself, once a minute at first, then less often, until the outcome is final. If Orderful reports the document as invalid, the Orderful block and the document show Failed at "Orderful validation", even though the Workato recipe itself ran correctly. The Workato block stays green because the recipe did its job.

Understanding statuses and 997s

Document statuses

StatusMeaning
ReceivedThe document has arrived and processing is starting.
In ProgressA step is running.
TransformedWorkato has converted the document to the next format.
RetryingA step is being tried again.
Sent to ERPAn inbound document has been handed to your ERP.
ERP AcknowledgedYour ERP confirmed it received the document.

The Ack column (997 acknowledgments)

A 997 is the partner's confirmation that it received your document, or your confirmation of theirs. The Ack column shows only the 997 state, so it is never confused with delivery.

RejectedThe 997 came back and rejected the document.
N/AThe document failed Orderful validation or could not be delivered, so no 997 is expected.

Chapter 8

Partners and expected EDI flows

The Partners page is where you set how each trading partner is tracked.

A partner appears here as soon as a document to or from it arrives.

Acknowledgment tracking

For each partner, choose whether to track:

  • the 997 we send for documents we receive (inbound), and
  • the 997 we expect back for documents we send (outbound).

Tracking drives the Ack column and the 997 reminder rules. Customer admins and support can change it.

Manage EDI Flow

An EDI flow describes the order of documents you expect with a partner, for example an 850 purchase order in, then an 855 acknowledgment, an 856 ship notice and an 810 invoice out. For each step you can set:

  • the document type and direction, and its place in the sequence;
  • whether one document produces several (one to many);
  • whether the step is optional;
  • how long after the previous step it counts as late, in minutes.

Track Document Flow

Enter a partner and a PO, shipment, invoice or BOL number. EDI360 gathers every related document, including the ship notice that quotes the PO and the invoice that quotes the shipment, and lays out each step of the flow:

Step resultMeaning
SuccessThe document was sent or received as expected.
FailedThe document failed.
Awaiting 997The document is through, but the acknowledgment has not come back.
PendingThe step has not happened yet and is not yet late.
LateThe step has not happened within the time set for it.
SkippedAn optional step that did not occur.

Track Document Flow is also available from the Transactions page.

Chapter 9

997 reminder rules

A rule emails the right people when a 997 is overdue, so nothing waits unnoticed.

The rules sit at the bottom of the Partners page. Customer admins create and change them. Support can view them.

What a rule says

FieldMeaning
PartnerOne partner, or all partners.
Document typesChosen types, or all.
DirectionInbound, outbound, or both (the default).
HoursHow long a 997 may stay pending before it counts as overdue, for example 24.
RecipientsUp to 10 email addresses.
WhenUp to two scheduled times a day, such as morning and evening, in your organisation's time zone, or on demand only.

What counts as pending

  • The document went through Orderful.
  • The partner's 997 is tracked in that direction.
  • Orderful has not reported the 997 as accepted or rejected.
  • The document did not fail validation or delivery.

The age counts from Orderful's creation time, or from when EDI360 first saw the document. The look-back is 30 days.

Using a rule

Open Partners and scroll to the 997 reminder rules.
Add a rule: choose the partner, types, direction, hours, recipients and times.
Use Preview to see which documents are pending right now, without sending anything.
Use Send now to email the list to the recipients straight away.

Scheduled emails go out only when something is waiting. Each scheduled time is sent once. The email goes through your organisation's own email account, which an administrator connects in Settings.

Chapter 10

Email alerts

Email alerts tell you when future documents succeed or fail. They are personal to you.

On the Transactions page, click Email Alerts.
Choose a partner (or any partner) and a document type (or any type).
Choose when to be told: on success, on failure, or both. Failure covers Failed and Warning.
Save. Use Send me a test email to check that email reaches you.
  • You are emailed once for each finished document that matches.
  • An alert only covers documents that finish after you create it.
  • You can switch an alert off, edit it or delete it at any time. Only you see your alerts.
Alerts or reminder rules? An email alert is a personal subscription to future documents. A 997 reminder rule is an organisation setting that reports overdue acknowledgments to a list of people. A report email (Chapter 12) tells someone about documents that already exist.
Chapter 11

Tickets in Jira or ServiceNow

Raise a ticket from a document, then follow it in EDI360 or in your ticket system. Both stay in step.

Creating a ticket

Open a document, or tick up to 25 documents on the Transactions page.
Click Create ticket.
Enter the reason and choose a priority: critical, high, medium or low.
Confirm. EDI360 creates the ticket and fills it in from the document: partners, status, where it stopped and why, Orderful and Workato identifiers, business facts and links. Raw EDI is not included.

The ticket then shows on each document page with its status. This needs your administrator to have connected Jira or ServiceNow in Settings.

The Tickets page

Every ticket raised from EDI360 is listed with its latest status, assignee and priority, and the documents it covers. Click a ticket to open its panel:

In the panelWhat it does
CommentsComments from both EDI360 and the ticket system, in order.
Post commentAdds a comment to the ticket. It is marked as posted from EDI360 with your name.
Change statusJira: moves the ticket along its workflow, offering only the moves allowed right now. ServiceNow: change the status in ServiceNow.
Open in Jira or Open in ServiceNowOpens the ticket in its own system.
RefreshReads the ticket again right now.

How fresh is the status?

EDI360 reads unfinished tickets automatically: every 5 minutes for the first day, every 30 minutes for the first week, then every 2 hours. Finished tickets are no longer checked. Opening or refreshing a ticket reads it immediately. In Settings, the ticketing card shows when the last sync ran.

Auditors do not see tickets.

Chapter 12

Emailing a document report

Send a clear, readable summary of documents to a colleague or a partner contact.

Open a document, or select documents on the Transactions page.
Click Send email.
Add up to 5 recipients, a subject and, if you like, a message.
Send. Each recipient receives one report. Replies come back to you.

What the report contains

  • A business section: document, partners, ship from and ship to, amounts, references and line items.
  • The three-block status for each document.
  • A technical section: Orderful and Workato identifiers, failed steps and links.

Raw EDI is never included. The report goes out through your organisation's own email account. Every send is recorded with its recipients.

Chapter 13

The AI assistant and Explain EDI

The assistant

Click the chat button at the bottom right of any page and ask in everyday language. The assistant answers from your organisation's own data, and asks you a short question when it needs a detail, such as which partner or which order.

Things you can ask

  • "Did we receive PO 100 from our partner?"
  • "Show failed 855s from the last 3 days."
  • "What are the item details and ship-to address for PO CHRP12345?"
  • "Which documents are still waiting for a 997 for more than 24 hours?"
  • "List our trading partners."
  • "What is the status of our open tickets?"

Asking it to do something

The assistant can prepare a 997 reminder rule, a ticket, an emailed report or a ticket comment. It does not carry these out by itself. It shows a card with the details, and nothing happens until you press Confirm. Press Cancel to drop it.

Good to know
AnswersCome from your documents, partners, 997 status and tickets. If nothing matches, it says so.
LinksDocument and ticket names in an answer are links you can click.
Raw EDIThe assistant does not show raw EDI.
ConversationIt remembers the current conversation only. Closing the chat starts a fresh one.
LimitsYour administrator sets a daily message limit for the organisation. If the assistant is switched off, the button does not appear.

Explain EDI

On a document page, Explain EDI writes the document in plain language: who sent it, to whom, what was ordered or shipped, and anything unusual. It is available to customer admins and support, in 12 languages.

Open the document.
Choose a language and click Explain EDI.
Read the result, or print it to PDF.

The explanation is saved, so opening it again is instant. Use the refresh option if you want it written again. Explain EDI needs the raw EDI to have been converted for that document.

The assistant and Explain EDI use the same AI model, which a customer admin chooses in Settings.

Chapter 14

Workato recipes

EDI360 learns from the logs your Workato recipes send. The Recipes page lists every recipe that has sent one.

Workato logs every recipe in the account, but EDI360 keeps only the EDI ones. Jobs that are not EDI are counted and set aside.

The Recipes list

ColumnMeaning
RecipeThe recipe name from Workato.
JobsHow many jobs have been recorded, and how many were set aside.
Keep jobsAuto (EDI360 decides), Always keep, or Ignore.
DirectionInbound or outbound, read from the log.
Document typeFor example 850, read from the log.

Changing how a recipe is read

Customer admins can change a recipe:

  • Choose Always keep for a recipe that is one stage of an EDI flow but contains no EDI of its own, for example SAP to a staging file or queue.
  • Choose Ignore for a recipe that should not appear.
  • Set the direction or document type when the automatic reading gets it wrong.

Saving also re-reads past jobs with the new setting. Jobs that were set aside earlier under Auto cannot be recovered; later ones are kept.

This page only changes how EDI360 reads the logs. It never changes anything in Workato.

Chapter 15

Test file generator

Build correct X12 test files for a trading partner. Customer admins and support.

Open Test files.
Choose the partner. Pick a saved partner, or enter one by hand. Partner details include the ISA and GS ids on both sides, the X12 version (4010 or 5010), test or production usage, delimiters, product-id order and default parties.
Choose the document: 850, 855, 856, 810, 204, 990, 214, 210, 940, 943, 944, 945 or 997. The form follows the document's layout, and the file previews live as you type, with warnings for anything missing.
Download. The download uses the partner's next control numbers and records the file in history. A preview never uses a number. You can also type control numbers yourself.

Line items

Paste rows from Excel or a CSV, with the columns matched by their header names. Save an .xlsx workbook as CSV first.

Partner rules

Each partner can carry rules, one per line, to match its specification:

RuleExample
Add a segment after anotherAFTER BEG: REF*DP*3
Replace a segmentREPLACE ITD: ITD**3***Net
Remove a segmentREMOVE PER
Add a noteA line starting with #

Generated files have a correctly sized ISA, matching segment, group and interchange counts, and consistent hierarchy.

Chapter 18

Cookbook: step-by-step recipes

Short, ready-to-follow recipes for the jobs that come up most often.

1. Check where a purchase order is

Roles: all  |  Where: Transactions
Open Transactions.
Type the PO number in Search and press Search. A number search covers all dates.
Open the 850. Read the three blocks and the status.
Use Track Document Flow to see the 855, 856 and 810 for the same PO.

2. Find out why a document failed

Roles: all (Explain EDI: customer admin, support)  |  Where: Document page
Open the failed document from Needs attention or the list with Status set to Failed.
Read the failure notice and find the first red block.
If Orderful failed it, click Open in Orderful to see the exact validation errors.
If you have access, use Explain EDI to read the document in plain words.

3. Answer a partner who says they never got a document

Roles: all  |  Where: Transactions
Search by the PO or document number.
Open the document and check the Orderful block, then the Delivery and Ack information.
For an outbound document, click Refresh from Orderful to get the latest status.
Use Send email to send the partner contact a report, or copy the details into your reply.

4. Chase missing 997s

Roles: customer admin (support can view)  |  Where: Partners
On Partners, confirm the partner's 997 tracking is on.
In the 997 reminder rules, add a rule: partner, hours (for example 24), recipients and times.
Click Preview to see what is pending right now.
Click Send now to email the list immediately, or let the schedule do it.

5. Be told when a partner's documents fail

Roles: all  |  Where: Transactions
Click Email Alerts.
Choose the partner and document type, and set the trigger to failure.
Save and use Send me a test email.

6. Raise a ticket for a failed document

Roles: customer admin, support, business  |  Where: Document page or Transactions
Open the document, or tick several on the list.
Click Create ticket, write the reason and choose the priority.
Confirm. The ticket appears on the document and on the Tickets page.

7. Update a ticket without opening Jira

Roles: customer admin, support, business  |  Where: Tickets
Open Tickets and click the ticket.
Read the comments, type yours and click Post comment.
For Jira, click Change status and choose the next step.

8. Start the day with a quick health check

Roles: all  |  Where: Dashboard
Open the Dashboard and look at the status cards.
Check Needs attention and open each failed or warning document.
Open Tickets to see which issues are already being worked.

9. Show only the partners you look after

Roles: all  |  Where: Transactions
Click My View.
Pick your partners and, for each, the document types.
Save. The Dashboard, list and exports now show only those. Click Show everything to clear it.

10. Send a status report to a colleague

Roles: customer admin, support, business  |  Where: Transactions
Filter the list, then tick the documents.
Click Send Email, add up to 5 recipients and a subject.
Send. Each person gets one report with business and technical sections.

11. Look something up with the assistant

Roles: all  |  Where: Chat button
Click the chat button.
Ask, for example: "What are the items and ship-to for PO 12345?"
Click the links in the answer to open the documents.

12. Keep a Workato stage in EDI360

Roles: customer admin  |  Where: Recipes
Open Recipes and find the recipe that moves a document to a staging file or queue.
Set Keep jobs to Always keep and save.
Later jobs from that recipe now appear as part of the document's journey.

13. Connect Jira or ServiceNow

Roles: customer admin  |  Where: Settings
Open Settings and the Ticketing card.
Choose the ticket system and enter its address, service account and token, plus the project or category details.
Click Test connection, then save.

14. Invite a new team member

Roles: customer admin  |  Where: Settings
In Members, enter the email, name and role.
Add. The invitation is emailed, and the link is shown so you can send it yourself.

15. Onboard a trading partner end to end

Roles: customer admin, support  |  Where: Partners, Test files, Transactions
Follow the six steps in Chapter 17.
Use Test files to build a test 850 or 856 if the partner needs a sample.
Filter Transactions to Test, trace each document and confirm the Ack column.
Add the 997 reminder rule and a failure alert before going to production.

Terms

TermMeaning
Inbound / OutboundInbound documents are received from a partner. Outbound documents are sent to a partner.
OrderfulThe EDI network that exchanges documents with your partners and validates them.
WorkatoThe integration platform that moves and converts documents between Orderful and your ERP, using recipes.
Recipe / jobA recipe is an automated workflow in Workato. A job is one run of a recipe.
ERPYour business system, for example SAP, that creates or receives the documents.
997A functional acknowledgment confirming that a document was received.
ISA / GSThe envelope segments of an EDI file. Their control numbers identify an interchange or group.
TransactionOne EDI document and its journey. EDI360 uses "document" and "transaction" for the same thing.
RunOne processing of a document. A retry adds another run to the same document.
EnvironmentTest or production.
Tenant / organisationOne customer's separate space in EDI360.
